# Platform Decision Log

Status terms: **Accepted** governs future work; **Proposed** needs approval; **Superseded** remains historical. All entries below are Accepted architecture baseline decisions. Implementation may be current or staged.

| ID | Title | Context and decision | Consequences | Review trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ADR-001 | One codebase, multiple publishers | Operate white-label publisher portals from one application and schema. | Tenant context is mandatory; avoid forks. | A regulatory or isolation need cannot be met in the shared design. |
| ADR-002 | Bluegate is the first tenant | Backfill existing records to a seeded Bluegate publisher. | Existing behavior and brand remain intact. | Business ownership of existing data changes. |
| ADR-003 | Students belong to a school | Every `Student` has a school; individual payment changes features, not academic ownership. | No unattached student accounts. | A direct-to-consumer product is approved. |
| ADR-004 | Placement uses enrollment | Class and section placement belongs in annual `StudentEnrollment`, not `Student`. | Promotion preserves history. | None; this is a core invariant. |
| ADR-005 | Book approval is annual | Adoption is scoped to school, academic year, class/section/subject, and book. | Renew each year; retain prior decisions. | Licensing policy changes. |
| ADR-006 | Book and premium access differ | Adoption/book entitlement answers content access; subscription answers premium learning features. | Separate checks and models. | Commercial packaging changes. |
| ADR-007 | Preview and full book differ | Public preview and protected full book are separate files and fields. | Public selects must omit full-book locations. | A new protected delivery format replaces PDFs. |
| ADR-008 | Resource audience is explicit | `TEACHER_ONLY`, `STUDENT`, or `BOTH` controls visibility; file type does not. | Add audience before student library. | More audience classes are required. |
| ADR-009 | AI is grounded | AI may use only approved, entitled book knowledge. | Reject ungrounded preparation; calls remain server-only. | A separately governed general AI product is approved. |
| ADR-010 | Preserve academic history | Deactivate/end-date records instead of rewriting prior years. | Restrictive relations and audited correction paths. | Retention policy requires lawful deletion. |
| ADR-011 | Teachers may hold both assignment types | A teacher can be class teacher and subject teacher across multiple sections/subjects. | Authorization evaluates each active assignment. | Assignment policy changes. |
| ADR-012 | Server resolves ownership | Identity, publisher, school, assignment, and enrollment derive from authenticated server context. | Browser IDs are only requested targets and must be re-scoped. | None. |
| ADR-013 | Super and Publisher Admin differ | Platform operations and tenant content administration are separate roles. | Support access is explicit and audited. | Operating model merges responsibilities. |
| ADR-014 | Staff uses permissions | Publisher staff receives permission profiles, not blanket admin access. | Introduce memberships, templates, and assignments. | Team size never warrants delegated access. |
| ADR-015 | Premium payer may vary | School or individual student may fund premium features. | Subscription records identify beneficiary and payer/source. | Sales policy standardizes one payer. |
| ADR-016 | Individual premium keeps school link | Individually subscribed students retain school and enrollment scope. | No duplicate student identity. | Direct-to-consumer exception is approved. |
| ADR-017 | Tutor assignment is explicit | Tutor access requires active `TutorAssignment` to named students and scope. | No automatic school-wide access. | Tutor operating model changes. |
| ADR-018 | Additive multi-tenancy | Add tenant roots, nullable keys, backfill, scope queries, then enforce constraints. | No big-bang migration or production reset. | A new empty deployment removes backfill needs. |
| ADR-019 | Visibility is not authorization | Navigation hiding improves UX; every server operation independently checks features and scope. | Test disabled-feature APIs directly. | None. |
| ADR-020 | Protected storage uses private objects | Full books and internal resources target private storage with short-lived signed access. | Current public Blob URLs are transitional. | Storage provider offers an equivalent stronger control. |
| ADR-021 | Computed entitlement with academic-year student grants | Accepted 13 July 2026: book entitlement is computed from authenticated tenant, academic assignment/enrollment, and annual adoption. Premium capability uses history-preserving `StudentAccessGrant` records scoped to one student and academic year; absence means `SCHOOL_BASIC`. | Premium activation never grants a book, and book adoption never grants premium features. School or individual sources share one deterministic strongest-plan resolver without payment fields. | Commercial rules require payer/subscription lifecycle, bulk school/class/section scope becomes too large for per-student grants, or access must span academic years. |
| ADR-022 | Automatic practice is deterministic and machine-gradable | Accepted 13 July 2026: automatic student practice selects only approved MCQ, True/False, and exact-normalized fill-in-the-blank questions whose stored structure can be validated and graded deterministically. Subjective types require a separately governed self-practice or human-review workflow. | The server excludes malformed and subjective questions, never uses fuzzy or AI grading, and reveals an approved answer/explanation only after that question is answered. | A reviewed structured answer format or an approved human-review workflow supports additional question types. |
| ADR-023 | Student AI is a guided chapter-scoped learning assistant | Accepted 13 July 2026; owner: Platform Architecture. Bluegate Student AI uses fixed learning modes and bounded Doubt Solver input because students need curriculum help, not unrestricted conversation. Every request and memory thread is scoped to publisher, school, student, academic year, entitled book, approved chapter, and approved knowledge. It uses no internet or generic-knowledge fallback. Student daily/monthly learning quota is publisher-controlled and independent of Teacher AI generation quota. | School Basic is locked; eligible premium plans also require the publisher Student AI feature. Changing chapters starts new memory. Safe refusals replace unsupported answers, and student-visible history never exposes prompts, providers, models, APIs, internal IDs, grounding metadata, or storage URLs. Voice, Image, Homework, and Whiteboard AI may later reuse the same authorization, grounding, quota, validation, and persistence contracts without creating global memory. | Approved policy permits cross-chapter tutoring, internet retrieval, a general chatbot, shared teacher/student quota, or a future modality cannot satisfy the same scoped contracts. |
| ADR-024 | Learning gaps are deterministic versioned educational signals | Accepted 13 July 2026; owner: Platform Architecture. Learning gaps derive only from trusted aggregated analytics under an explicit policy version. Every occurrence retains evidence and academic scope; it is not a medical diagnosis, fixed-ability label, prediction, or remedial assignment. | Reading, revision, and AI use are contextual only. Scored coverage is mandatory, student wording is supportive, role views are scoped, closed history remains immutable, and Phase 9.10 must govern any later intervention separately. | Validated pedagogy changes thresholds, a new analytics dimension is approved, or remedial governance requires a revised evidence contract. |
| ADR-025 | Remedial recommendations are deterministic drafts requiring teacher activation | Accepted 13 July 2026; owner: Platform Architecture. Phase 9.10 maps Phase 9.9 gaps only to existing entitled content references under `BG-REMEDIAL-1.0`. Automatic work stops at a versioned draft; a scoped teacher review is the publication boundary. | No generated remediation, raw-response inspection, recommendation editing, or automatic student publication. Steps and reviews are auditable, and aggregate effectiveness uses real completions and resolved gaps only. | Validated pedagogy changes ordering/due policy, approved content gains explicit worksheet or mentor types, or governance approves a different publication authority. |
| ADR-026 | Public onboarding creates pending relationships, never authority | Accepted 14 July 2026; owner: Platform Architecture. Public School and Teacher requests create pending records under server-derived tenant/school targets. Student activation may only attach Auth.js credentials to an existing school-owned Student using an expiring single-use hashed code. | Existing accounts migrate approved. Publisher Admin approves schools; School approves teacher associations; teaching assignments stay separate. Pending, rejected, suspended, expired, duplicate, and cross-scope requests fail closed. | Trusted domain onboarding, multiple publisher selection, invitation-only school provisioning, or a different student login identifier is approved. |
| ADR-027 | Mentor authority is an explicit current-year student assignment | Accepted 16 July 2026; owner: Platform Architecture. A distinct Mentor identity may view safe learning projections only for named students with an active assignment, current enrollment, enabled Publisher Mentor feature, and active Individual Premium with Mentor plan. One active PRIMARY Mentor is allowed per student/year. | Assignment replacement ends old authority while preserving history. Notes and activities are append-only. Mentor remedial actions are recommendations only, Student AI launch is an audited handoff, and no school-wide discovery, academic mutation, messaging, video, or calling is permitted. | Consent or commercial policy permits supporting mentors, a parent-request workflow, school-wide mentor scope, direct Mentor AI conversation, or Mentor authority to change academic evidence. |

| ADR-028 | Parent access is explicit, approved, and read-focused | Accepted 16 July 2026; owner: Platform Architecture. Parent authority comes only from a named, school-reviewed `ParentStudentRelationship`; matching email, phone, surname, address, or other contact data never creates authority. Invitation activation creates a pending relationship, and a separate School approval enables released child-learning reads. | Revocation ends access without deleting history. Parent projections are narrower than or equal to Student release visibility and exclude answers, protected content, private Mentor notes, AI conversations, administrative data, and academic mutations. Publisher `PARENT_PORTAL` gating is evaluated per linked child. | Approved consent, family-contact, cross-publisher identity, subscription-management, communications, or child-data privacy policy requires a different authority boundary. |

New decisions use the next ID and include status, context, decision, consequences, owner/date, and review trigger.
