# Purpose

The SARTHI Marketplace provides a secure digital ecosystem where educational organizations and creators can publish, discover, purchase, license, subscribe to, and distribute educational content and services.

The Marketplace is designed to promote quality educational resources while respecting intellectual property, licensing, and institutional policies.

---

# Vision

The Marketplace shall become India's largest trusted education marketplace, connecting publishers, schools, teachers, universities, coaching institutes, educational creators, and learners through one secure platform.
