# SARTHI Student Platform Specification

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Module:** Student Platform

**Product:** SARTHI

**Owner:** Edora Learning Pvt. Ltd.

---

# Purpose

The Student Platform provides learners with a personalized digital learning environment that supports classroom learning, self-study, assessments, skill development, collaboration, and lifelong educational growth.

It is designed to encourage curiosity, mastery, independent learning, and responsible use of technology.

---

# Vision

The Student Platform shall become every learner's personal educational companion from primary school through higher education and lifelong learning.

---

# Assignments

Students can:

- View assignments
- Submit work
- Upload files
- View deadlines
- Receive teacher feedback
- Track submission history

---

# Practice

Students may:

- Solve practice questions
- Attempt quizzes
- Complete worksheets
- Practice previous mistakes
- Retry incorrect answers

Practice should encourage mastery rather than memorization.

---

# Assessments

Students can:

- Take quizzes
- Take tests
- Participate in examinations
- View results
- Review feedback
- Track improvements

High-stakes assessments follow institutional policies.

---

# AI Tutor

The AI Tutor may assist students by:

- Explaining concepts
- Providing examples
- Answering questions
- Recommending practice
- Creating revision plans
- Generating quizzes
- Explaining mistakes
- Encouraging self-learning

The AI Tutor should never complete assignments on behalf of students.
