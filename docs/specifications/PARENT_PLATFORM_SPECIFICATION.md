# SARTHI Parent Platform Specification

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Module:** Parent Platform

**Product:** SARTHI

**Owner:** Edora Learning Pvt. Ltd.

---

# Purpose

The Parent Platform enables parents and guardians to stay informed about their child's academic progress, attendance, wellbeing, communication, and school activities while supporting collaboration between home and school.

The platform is designed to provide meaningful insights rather than overwhelming parents with excessive information.

---

# Vision

The Parent Platform shall become the trusted digital bridge connecting parents, students, teachers, and institutions throughout a learner's educational journey.

---

# Primary Users

- Parents
- Guardians
- Authorized Family Members

Each user accesses only students they are authorized to manage.

# Child Management

Parents can:

- View linked children
- Switch between children
- View academic profile
- View class and section
- View teachers
- View timetable

Parents cannot modify institutional records.

---

# Academic Progress

Parents may view:

- Subject-wise progress
- Competency reports
- Learning goals
- Skill development
- Teacher observations
- Improvement trends

The focus is on progress over time rather than comparison with other students.

---

# Attendance

Parents may:

- View daily attendance
- View monthly attendance
- Receive absence notifications
- Submit leave requests (if enabled)

Attendance records remain under institutional control.

---

# Homework

Parents can:

- View assigned homework
- Track completion
- View submission status
- Receive reminders
- Access supporting resources

Homework remains the student's responsibility.

---

# Assessments

Parents may view:

- Upcoming tests
- Results
- Teacher feedback
- Competency reports
- Performance trends

Assessment integrity must be maintained.

---

# Calendar

Parents can access:

- School events
- Parent-teacher meetings
- Examination schedules
- Holidays
- Important deadlines

---

# Communication

Parents may communicate with:

- Teachers
- School Administration

Communication follows institutional policies.

---

# Fee Management

Where enabled, parents may:

- View fee statements
- Download receipts
- Make online payments
- Track payment history

Payment functionality depends on institutional configuration.

---

# Reports

Parents can access:

- Report cards
- Progress reports
- Attendance summaries
- Learning analytics
- Achievement records

Reports should be easy to understand and printable.

---

# Resources

Parents may access:

- Parenting guides
- Study tips
- School circulars
- Institution-approved learning resources

---

# AI Parent Assistant

The AI Parent Assistant may:

- Explain report cards
- Suggest study routines
- Recommend learning resources
- Interpret competency reports
- Provide parenting guidance related to education
- Answer platform questions

The AI Assistant must not replace teachers or provide medical, legal, or psychological advice.

---

# Notifications

Parents receive notifications for:

- Attendance updates
- Homework deadlines
- Assessment schedules
- New report cards
- School announcements
- Fee reminders
- Emergency alerts

Notification preferences should be configurable.

---

# Parent Profile

Profiles include:

- Personal information
- Contact details
- Linked children
- Communication preferences
- Notification settings
- Identity verification status

---

# Permissions

Parents may only access:

- Authorized student records
- Institution-approved information
- Personal payment records
- Their own communication history

All access is governed by institutional permissions.

---

# Accessibility

The Parent Platform should support:

- Mobile-first design
- Responsive layouts
- Large touch targets
- Screen readers
- High contrast mode
- Adjustable text size

---

# Success Metrics

The Parent Platform should improve:

- Parent engagement
- Home-school communication
- Homework completion
- Attendance awareness
- Parent satisfaction

---

# Future Enhancements

Future capabilities may include:

- AI-powered learning recommendations
- Family learning plans
- Multiple guardian workflows
- Voice-based assistant
- Regional language support
- Smart reminders
- Wellness insights
- Community events

---

# Guiding Principle

The Parent Platform exists to strengthen collaboration between families and educational institutions while respecting student independence, teacher expertise, and institutional policies.
