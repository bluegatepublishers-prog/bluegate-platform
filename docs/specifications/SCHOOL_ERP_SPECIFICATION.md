# Purpose

The School ERP provides a complete digital operating system for educational institutions, managing academic, administrative, financial, communication, and operational processes from a unified platform.

The system is designed to reduce paperwork, improve transparency, automate repetitive tasks, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

# Vision

The School ERP shall become the central operating platform for schools, enabling administrators, teachers, students, and parents to work together efficiently while maintaining institutional autonomy and security.

- Its own students
- Its own staff
