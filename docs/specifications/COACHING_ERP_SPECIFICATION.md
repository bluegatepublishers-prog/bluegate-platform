# Purpose

The Coaching ERP provides a complete digital operating system for coaching institutes, tuition centers, skill academies, competitive examination institutes, and training organizations.

It manages admissions, batches, faculty, courses, examinations, communication, finance, and analytics through a unified platform.

---

# Vision

The Coaching ERP shall become the preferred platform for coaching institutes by simplifying administration while enhancing teaching quality and student outcomes.
