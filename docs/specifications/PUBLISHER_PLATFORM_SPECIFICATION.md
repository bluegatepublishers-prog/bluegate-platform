**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Module:** Publisher Platform

**Owner:** Edora Learning Pvt. Ltd.

---

# Purpose

The Publisher Platform enables educational publishers to manage their organization, books, digital resources, curriculum mapping, schools, teachers, sales insights, and AI-assisted publishing from a unified workspace.

The platform supports publishers of all sizes while maintaining strict tenant isolation and data security.

---

# Vision

The Publisher Platform shall become the complete digital operating system for educational publishers, supporting the entire publishing lifecycle from content creation to classroom adoption.

# Security

Publishers may only access:

- Their own tenant
- Their own users
- Their own books
- Their own resources
- Their own analytics

Cross-tenant access is prohibited.

---

# Audit

The following actions must generate audit events:

- Book publication
- Resource publication
- Team changes
- Permission changes
- Branding updates
- Organization settings
- Inspection decisions

Audit logs are immutable.
