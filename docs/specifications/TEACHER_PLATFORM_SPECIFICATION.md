**Version:** 1.0

**Owner:** Edora Learning Pvt. Ltd.

---

# Purpose

The Teacher Platform provides educators with a complete digital workspace for planning lessons, managing classrooms, assessing learners, collaborating with schools, accessing educational resources, and using Artificial Intelligence responsibly to improve teaching effectiveness.

The platform is designed to reduce administrative workload and allow teachers to focus on teaching and student learning.

---

# Vision

The Teacher Platform shall become the primary digital workspace for teachers throughout their professional careers.

A teacher should be able to complete nearly every academic task from one dashboard.
