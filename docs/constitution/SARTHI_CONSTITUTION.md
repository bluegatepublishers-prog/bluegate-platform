# SARTHI Constitution

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Product:** SARTHI

**Tagline:** Guiding Every Learner

**Product Owner:** Edora Learning Pvt. Ltd.

---

# Preamble

We believe that education is a lifelong journey rather than a series of isolated academic stages.

SARTHI is created to provide every learner, educator, institution, and publisher with a secure, intelligent, and connected education ecosystem.

The purpose of SARTHI is not merely to digitize education but to improve learning, teaching, collaboration, assessment, and lifelong growth through technology.

Every architectural, technical, and product decision shall align with this Constitution.

---

# Article 1
## Product Identity

SARTHI is the flagship Education Operating System developed and owned by Edora Learning Pvt. Ltd.

SARTHI is an independent platform.

It is not owned by any publisher, school, university, coaching institute, or government organization.

Bluegate Publishers is the first enterprise customer of SARTHI.

Future publishers, institutions, and organizations shall receive equal platform capabilities.

---

# Article 2
## Platform Vision

SARTHI shall become a unified Education Operating System connecting:

- Learners
- Teachers
- Parents
- Schools
- Coaching Institutes
- Colleges
- Universities
- Publishers
- Educational Content Creators
- Skill Development Organisations
- Corporate Learning Organisations
- Government Educational Bodies

through one intelligent platform.

---

# Article 3
## One Person • One Identity

Every person shall receive one permanent SARTHI Universal ID (SUID).

The SUID:

- belongs to the individual
- never changes
- is never reused
- survives institution changes
- supports multiple educational and professional roles throughout life

Example:

Student

↓

College Student

↓

Teacher

↓

Principal

↓

Author

↓

Mentor

↓

Researcher

The identity remains the same.

---

# Article 4
## Identity Verification

Identity and trust are different concepts.

A SUID represents identity.

Trust is built progressively.

Verification may include:

- Mobile verification
- Email verification
- Parent verification
- Institution verification
- Publisher verification
- Professional verification
- Government-linked educational identifiers (optional)
- Manual verification
- Long-term platform history

SARTHI shall never depend upon one identifier alone.

---

# Article 5
## Lifelong Learning Passport

Every learner owns a Learning Passport.

The Learning Passport follows the learner throughout life.

It may contain:

- Courses
- Books
- Learning history
- Assessments
- Skills
- Competencies
- Projects
- Portfolios
- Certificates
- Achievements
- AI Insights
- Teacher Feedback
- Verified Credentials

The learner owns the passport.

Institutions contribute records but do not own the lifelong identity.

---

# Article 6
## Lifelong Professional Passport

Every educator owns a Professional Passport.

It may contain:

- Qualifications
- Experience
- Institutions Served
- Courses Taught
- Resources Published
- Research
- Certifications
- Marketplace Contributions
- Reviews
- Awards
- AI Contributions

The educator retains this profile throughout their career.

---

# Article 7
## Multi-Tenant Platform

SARTHI is designed as a secure multi-tenant platform.

Supported tenants include:

- Publishers
- Schools
- Coaching Institutes
- Colleges
- Universities
- NGOs
- Corporate Learning
- Independent Teachers
- Individual Learners

Every tenant operates independently.

Tenant data shall remain isolated unless explicitly authorised.

---

# Article 8
## Publisher Neutrality

SARTHI shall remain publisher-neutral.

Every publisher receives equal capabilities.

Bluegate Publishers is the first enterprise customer but shall not receive architectural privileges that prevent future expansion.

---

# Article 9
## Institution Independence

Educational records belong to the institution that creates them.

The lifelong educational identity belongs to the individual.

Students and educators retain continuity while changing institutions.

---

# Article 10
## Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence exists to assist—not replace—human educators.

AI may:

- Generate
- Explain
- Recommend
- Evaluate
- Analyse
- Translate
- Personalise

Final educational and administrative authority always remains with authorised humans.

# Article 12
## Resource Ecosystem

Teachers, publishers, institutions, and creators may publish educational resources.

Each resource shall define:

- Owner
- Licence
- Visibility
- Permissions
- Version
- Curriculum Mapping

Resources may be:

- Private
- Institution Only
- Publisher Network
- Marketplace
- Public

---

# Article 13
## Marketplace

SARTHI shall support a trusted educational marketplace.

Marketplace participants may include:

- Publishers
- Teachers
- Institutions
- Universities
- Independent Creators

Marketplace content shall respect copyright, licensing, and ownership.

---

# Article 14
## Learning Analytics

SARTHI measures learning rather than marks alone.

Analytics may include:

- Mastery
- Competency
- Progress
- Improvement
- Engagement
- Learning Patterns
- Skill Development

Analytics should guide improvement rather than merely report performance.

---

# Article 15
## Accessibility

Every interface shall be:

- Simple
- Mobile Friendly
- Accessible
- Inclusive
- Easy for first-time users

Complex workflows should be broken into guided steps.

---

# Article 16
## Privacy and Security

Every individual controls their identity.

Personal information shall be protected through appropriate security measures.

Users shall have transparency regarding how their information is used.

The platform shall support secure authentication, role-based access control, audit logging, and responsible data governance.

---

# Article 17
## Open Integration

SARTHI shall support integration with:

- Government education systems
- APAAR (where supported and with appropriate authorization)
- Publisher systems
- School ERP
- University ERP
- AI Providers
- Payment Systems
- External APIs

External identifiers complement the SARTHI Universal ID and do not replace it.

---

# Article 18
## Design Philosophy

Technology should simplify education.

Every screen should answer one question:

"What is the next best action for this user?"

If a user requires extensive training to use a feature, the feature should be redesigned.

---

# Article 19
## Continuous Innovation

SARTHI shall evolve continuously while preserving backward compatibility wherever practical.

New technologies—including AI, immersive learning, and future educational innovations—should strengthen the platform without compromising its constitutional principles.

---

# Article 20
## Constitutional Supremacy

This Constitution is the highest guiding document for SARTHI.

All future:

- Product Requirements Documents
- Architecture Documents
- Database Designs
- API Standards
- User Experience Designs
- Marketplace Policies
- AI Policies
- Security Standards

shall conform to the principles defined herein.
