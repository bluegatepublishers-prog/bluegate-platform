# Executive Summary

SARTHI is a cloud-native Education Operating System (Education OS) designed to connect every learner, educator, institution, publisher, and educational creator through one secure and intelligent platform.

Unlike traditional Learning Management Systems (LMS) or School ERP software, SARTHI combines learning, teaching, assessment, publishing, administration, collaboration, analytics, and artificial intelligence into a single integrated ecosystem.

SARTHI is designed as a multi-tenant platform capable of serving educational publishers, schools, coaching institutes, colleges, universities, independent teachers, learners, parents, and training organizations while maintaining complete organizational isolation and data security.

The platform is intended to support a learner throughout their educational and professional journey by providing a lifelong digital identity, continuous learning records, intelligent recommendations, and secure educational services.
